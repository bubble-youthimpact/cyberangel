Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026
CyberAngel does not collect any personal information from its users.
1

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up or login, and we never ask for or collect personal information such as your name, contact details, email or location.

2

Information stored on your device

Your best game scores and settings (large text, vibration) are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. They are deleted when you delete the app or tap 'Clear game records' in Settings.

3

Third parties and ads

None. We don't use ads, analytics tools or external servers, and we never share any information with third parties.

4

Phone calls

Tapping a number on the Report & Help screen opens your device's phone app. The app does not access your calls or call history.

5

Children's privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will let you know through an app update.
